# v6.4: Ruch, and the works speaking first (20 Oct 2026)

Two changes. Both are tries the owner judges with his hands. Neither is a finished language for Studio.

## Why

On 20 Oct the owner asked for a whole look at where Jaskinia is going. I looked at what he sees first and at what he has asked of agents for months. Three findings, each measured, not guessed:

- **His screens speak the workshop's process.**
  - On Przegląd, 39% of the visible lines are commits, ages and branches.
  - On Granie the share was 38%. The shelf of games said "commit 3 dni temu" under each game. The launch card said "ostatni commit", "Od poprzedniego zrzutu", "Stan według repo" and "Ostatnio ruszane gałęzie" before anything about the game.
- **Four of the five starting layouts are archive views.** Nothing in them serves making.
- **One need kept coming back across his projects.** He wanted to give his own parts their axes and their motion by hand. Instead, agents tuned pivots by numbers, and the result still looked wrong to him.
  - His Blockbench models are mechanical parts: wheels, gears that carry a cube named `Axis`, pistons, propellers, tracks. Most of their groups keep Blockbench's default pivots.

The process lesson from earlier releases also applies: let him feel an interaction early, and harden it after.

## Ruch (Materiały → Ruch)

A switch on the stage of any model. With it on:

| He does | Jaskinia does |
|---|---|
| clicks a cube | chooses the part he most likely means (below); the path in his file (`gear › zab`) lets him choose another level |
| Ctrl+clicks cubes | gathers them into a part of his own (a group in the file is not always the part he means) |
| says **Obraca się** or **Przesuwa się** | proposes the axis and pivot, and the part shows its motion once |
| drags a moving part | moves it: a turn follows the hand around the pivot like a dial, a slide follows the hand along the axis within its travel; the view does not turn meanwhile |
| **Pokaż ruch** / **Zatrzymaj** | everything moves at once / everything goes back to the exact rest pose |
| **Plik dla agentów** | saves the motion as JSON (or copies it) |

**Which part a click chooses.** The rule follows how he builds wheels in his own files, which I read privately on 20 Oct. Nothing of them is in this repository.
- A group that holds a loose cube named like an axis is one wheel with its shaft.
  - Inside it, a group with an axis of its own is another wheel and stays out. The click then takes the wheel's own loose cubes, named after its group.
  - A group without an axis, like the teeth beside the shaft, belongs to the wheel.
- A group Blockbench named by itself (`group`, `cube`, `octagon`…) is passed over for the nearest name of his. When such a group is chosen anyway, his name goes in front: `zębatki › group`.
- Otherwise the click takes the group that holds the cube.

**Skinned glTF exports.** Blockbench can export a model as glTF with its groups as an armature. Several of his Box3D models in Materiały are made that way: one skinned mesh, every group a bone.
- A click there chooses the bone that moves most of the clicked face.
- A part is that bone, and its shape is the vertices it moves.
- Empty bones named like an axis are points on the axis. Two of them with one stem (`Axis_WheelSpin_A` and `_B`) make the axis line.
- Moving a bone moves its vertices; the rest of the mesh stays.

**How the axis is proposed.**
- First choice: a cube named like an axis (`Axis`, `axle`, `shaft`, `oś`, `wał`, `pin`, `sworzeń`, `bolt`), inside the part or loose beside it in the same group. Its middle is the pivot. Its long side is the axis only when that side is clearly the longest (1.3 times the next). A plate named Axis, 16 by 15, gives its middle but not its direction.
- In a skinned export, two empty axis bones with one stem give the line. Two different stems (`Axis_Front`, `Axis_Rear`) are two axles, not one line.
- Otherwise the shape decides:
  - a turn goes about the part's thinnest side, through its middle (a wheel);
  - a slide goes along its longest side, half its length by default (a piston).

**What he can change with one click each.**
- Axis X/Y/Z, in the file's own frame and in Blockbench's colours. He can also reverse it.
- The pivot:
  - the axis cube;
  - the middle;
  - the group's own Blockbench pivot, offered only when it sits on the part;
  - a cube he points at.
- The range of a turn: endless, ±30°, ±90°, 0–90°.
- The travel of a slide: ¼, ½ or all of the part's length.

**Hints.** Names in his file hint, as a dashed suggestion and never a choice made for him:
- a turn for wheel, koło, gear, zębatka, propeller, śmigło, rotor and similar names;
- a slide for piston, tłok, rod and similar names.

**Kept, and carried forward.**
- His motion is kept on the material record (`m.ruch`), changed in one transaction like every material change since v6.3.
- It survives a reload. The window remembers that Ruch was on.
- A new version of the same file takes the motion along by the names of its groups and cubes, so it holds even when cubes were added before them. The panel says what came along.
- Wejdź turns Ruch off: inside, the model stands as in the file.

**Only kinematics.** Nothing here is physics, joints with limits in a solver, inverse kinematics, or an export to an engine. The rest pose is the file's. Nesting composes: a gear on a turning carrier turns with it.

### For agents

- `Jaskinia.materialy.ruch(id)` returns `{ jaskinia: 'ruch v1', co, material, parts }`, or `null` when he set nothing.
- Each part is `{ name, kind: 'obrót' | 'przesuw', axis, pivot, range, skad: { os, punkt }, nodes, names }`. The fields:
  - **axis and pivot:** in the file's frame (metres, Y up, 16 Blockbench pixels = 1 m), before the material's default turn and scale;
  - **range:** radians or metres of the file; `null` for an endless turn;
  - **nodes and names:** the part's place in the loaded model, by child index and by name;
  - **skad:** where the axis and the pivot came from (`klocek:Axis`, `bryla` the shape, `reka` his click, `srodek`, `plik`, `wskazany`).
- `Jaskinia.materialy.rig(object, id)` drives a fresh copy from `Jaskinia.materialy.object(id)`:
  - `set(name | index, value)` moves one part;
  - `rest()` puts the copy back as in the file.
- "Dla agentów" (`#agenci`) lists the motion in words next to each material.
- The motion is the owner's decision about his own parts. Read it before guessing a pivot, and do not overwrite it. Propose changes to him.

## The works speak first

- **The "Do zagrania" shelf.** Each game says what it is: his own sentence when he fixed one in the Library, else Claude's hypothesis, which the tooltip says is a proposal. The game's own name is not repeated in front of the sentence. When it was last touched is on hover.
- **A game's launch card.** It opens with the game's sentence, then Zagraj and the address. The workshop's process is folded under "Z warsztatu agentów", one click away:
  - life;
  - last commit;
  - changes since the previous snapshot;
  - the repo's own state note;
  - recent branches.

Measured on the artifact host, Granie went from 38% to 31% process lines. Most of what remains is in the Stan and Rytm windows that the Granie preset places. His saved layouts were not touched. Changing what the starting layouts hold is a decision for him.

## Checks

`tools/jk64_test.mjs` has 56 checks in real Chromium on three made-up models:
- a Blockbench machine: a base, a gear with an `Axis` cube, a piston, and a wheel whose group also holds its mudguard;
- a Blockbench file of wheels built the way he builds them: loose gear cubes beside other wheels' groups, teeth in a default-named group beside a shaft, an Axis plate;
- a skinned glTF wheel: a tire and a socket on two bones, with two empty axis bones.

The checks cover:
- the first click on each kind of wheel, the skinned one included, and the axis it gets;
- a real click and the path in the file;
- the axis from the cube;
- the motion shown at once, then the exact rest pose (matrix difference 0);
- a real dial drag of a quarter turn, with the camera unmoved;
- a drag on empty ground still turns the view;
- the piston pulled to the end of its travel;
- a real Ctrl+click part that leaves the mudguard standing;
- the "z pliku" pivot offered only when it sits on the part;
- show and stop back to the exact rest pose;
- `ruch()` and `rig()`;
- the agents window;
- a reload;
- a record rewritten by an older build without his motion, which gets it back from the mirror after a reload;
- a new version with every path moved, carried by names;
- Wejdź;
- phone width;
- the shelf and the launch card.

## Open

- What he feels when he drags his own gears and pistons is the real test. The numbers here only make sure nothing is lost.
- Relations he chooses himself. Today only the file's own nesting composes. Making, removing and re-making relations is the freedom that building needs, more than editing the points of a fixed mechanism. The likely next step is a part that rides on another part he points at.
- On a phone Ruch can be used, but a part of his own needs Ctrl.
- The motion is not played outside Ruch, and not while walking inside.
- Places and Studio do not use it yet. A place built from a material could pin the motion it was built with, the way it pins settings revisions.
