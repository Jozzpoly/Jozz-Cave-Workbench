# Jaskinia v6.2: the owner's remarks, answered by behaviour (18 Oct 2026, evening)

On 18 Oct the owner used v6 for an afternoon and left ten remarks through "To mnie uwiera". He sent them as a package.
Every one of them was right, and together they show one problem under the separate bugs: **Jaskinia did not follow
his hands and did not answer him the same way twice.** The wheel button did one thing in 3D and another on the wall.
One chart answered the pointer, the next one waited for the browser's late tooltip, and the map flashed. A game opened
in a new window every time and never got the keyboard. His Blockbench exports looked like someone else's models.

v6.2 answers each remark with behaviour and writes down the conventions, so that the next agent keeps them.

## The conventions (the owner's hands)

These are stable rules for every agent and every window. They are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and in the compass.

- **Mouse in 3D.**
  - Left button: turn around the model.
  - Wheel button (and right button): always moves the view (pan).
  - Turning the wheel: zooms toward the cursor.
  - The wheel button never starts the browser's autoscroll over a stage.
- **Walking and flying** (Minecraft and Unreal):
  - WASD walks.
  - Space jumps; when flying, Space goes up and Shift goes down. On foot, Shift runs.
  - F toggles flying.
  - The wheel sets the speed (×0.25 to ×8).
  - A key that is let go is let go, whatever else is held. A browser shortcut (Ctrl, Alt, Meta) clears every held key, and so does leaving the tab.
  - "Pełny ekran" in the walk holds the keyboard (Keyboard Lock, Chromium browsers): Ctrl+W cannot close the tab, and holding Esc leaves.
- **Games.** A plain click on "Zagraj" switches the game in the game window: the focused one, else the last used, else the last one open. Shift+click opens a new game window beside. Ctrl+click (or the wheel button) opens a browser tab. A game window that is opened or taken into use gets the keyboard, so WASD and the arrows go to the game and not to Jaskinia.
- **Pointing answers the same way everywhere.** Every chart mark answers with the same note (`tip()`):
  - the mark brightens;
  - the note follows the pointer, with the value large and the day or hour under it;
  - it fades back on leaving.

  Nothing flashes. SVG `<title>`s are upgraded to this note automatically. A chart with its own richer note (the map, Puls, Statystyki) marks itself `data-own-tip`.
- **Pixels.** Blockbench models are pixel art. Texture pixels are sharp by default, with Blockbench's sampler: nearest, no mipmaps. One switch covers every model: "Ostre piksele" on the Materiały stage and Ustawienia → Wygląd → Modele 3D. When the stage draws fewer pixels than the screen, it enlarges them block by block instead of blurring.
- **Compact on a desk.** With a mouse the interface is compact by itself, and window tools stay quiet until the header is pointed at. Large controls only on touch.
- **Only his models.** Before offering a model as his, check who made it (the file's history).

**Answers to remarks.** They live in the private layer (`private/uwagi_odpowiedzi.json`, keyed by the remark's
moment) and `tools/render.py` puts them only into the private build. When an agent acts on a remark, it adds an answer
there. The owner then sees what happened right where he wrote it, and every agent sees which remarks are still open.

## Checks

`node tools/jk62_test.mjs` runs 38 checks:
- charts: no `<title>` left on a mark; the year, the clock, Puls and Statystyki answer with the same note and light; one note over the map;
- remarks: correcting, another tab taking it in, answers in the private build (none in a public one), "Dla agentów";
- models: the Blockbench look; sharp pixels by default and the switch both ways; the offer without the Box3D meshes; letting them go once with Cofnij. If a folder of Blockbench `.obj` exports is at hand (`JK_BB_EXPORT`), every model in it is checked as well;
- controls: pan on the wheel button without zoom; fullscreen offered in the walk; flying up and down; speed on the wheel; no stuck key;
- chrome: compact with a mouse, quiet tools until the header is pointed at.

The older suites stay green (counts in [VERSIONS.md](VERSIONS.md)).
